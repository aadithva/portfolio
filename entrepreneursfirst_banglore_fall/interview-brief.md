## The Strategic Frame

Use Machiavellian principles in the useful, ethical sense: understand the
interviewer's incentives, anticipate attacks, and control the order in which true
facts are understood.

### Frame to Establish

- Not: "A designer who has tried many unrelated ideas."
- Instead: "A product founder repeatedly solving the human handoff around complex
  technology."
- Not: "An AI-video company looking for another market."
- Instead: "A team that learned video generation was commoditizing and moved its
  workflow advantage into a high-stakes use case prompted by a healthcare contact."
- Not: "People seemed interested."
- Instead: "More than 10 clinician conversations, one active IQRAA pilot, and one
  approved Stanford collaboration, with the limitations stated clearly."
- Not: "I might leave Microsoft."
- Instead: "The team has exact full-time transition dates."

### Costly Signals

Claims of ambition are weak. Actions with opportunity cost are strong:

1. Three founders are leaving stable jobs.
2. The same team has already spent a year building and selling together.
3. Aadith continued after a YC rejection and changed the market rather than
   abandoning the accumulated capability.
4. The team entered real clinical workflows rather than remaining at demo level.
5. Aadith and the team are preparing to relocate to Bangalore to work together
   full-time after the fixed August transition dates.

## Biography Compression Rule

The raw founder story contains many useful details, but the opening is not a
chronological autobiography. Use only four beats:

1. Early startup ownership during IIT Guwahati.
2. Trust and scale learning on M365 Copilot.
3. Owly Studio learning and customer pull into healthcare.
4. Current Medora proof and fixed full-time transition.

Cycling, Inter-IIT, relocation, late-night US outreach, and cofounder achievements
are supporting stories. Use one only when the question asks for it.

## Five Attacks to Pre-Empt

### 1. "You are not from healthcare."

State this before it is weaponized:

> I do not claim clinical expertise. I own the translation from approved clinical
> information to a trusted patient experience. Clinicians own medical correctness;
> our system gives them review and approval control.

### 2. "This is another pivot."

> The markets changed, but the learning compounded: models commoditize, customers
> pay for workflow and outcomes, narrative determines whether information is used,
> and discretionary advertising was a weaker pain than patient preparation.

### 3. "You are not committed while employed."

> Hari and Adi become full-time August 1. I finish two years at Microsoft and leave
> at the end of August. These are fixed transition dates, not intentions.

### 4. "You already have a team, so why EF?"

> EF can pressure-test my individual founder edge, increase peer density, compress
> commercial learning, and expand the network. I will not pretend I am casually
> shopping for a replacement team.

### 5. "Your evidence is still weak."

> Correct. IQRAA proves entry into a real clinical workflow, and Stanford proves
> institutional collaboration. Neither proves repeatable revenue or patient
> outcomes. The next job is to produce those results.

## Answer Structure

Every answer should fit this sequence:

1. **Claim:** Answer the actual question in one sentence.
2. **Proof:** Give one or two specific pieces of evidence.
3. **Implication:** Explain why that evidence matters.
4. **Stop:** Let Divit choose the next branch.

Target length:

- ordinary answer: 20-35 seconds;
- founder story: 45-60 seconds;
- pressure follow-up: 10-20 seconds.

## Questions to Ask Divit

1. "For founders with a strong product edge but limited initial domain tenure, what

## Phrases to Avoid

- "Stanford is running our pilot."
- "All the clinicians loved it."
- "We have validated the market."
- "There are no competitors."
- "Healthcare is a bigger TAM, so we pivoted."
- "I plan to leave Microsoft soon."
- "We have spoken to 100-200 doctors."
- "We have delivered patient videos at IQRAA" until the exact patient-level
  delivery evidence is confirmed.
- Exact funding, investor, user-count, or enterprise-client names from Aadith's
  earlier employers unless independently verified and safe to disclose.
- Any precise Owly revenue, customer-count, cycling, or ranking claim that has not
  been checked against `evidence-ledger.md`.
