## Advertised geometry and materials

The creator's [listing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/crt-computer-monitor-f2ff0013f86e4cd0a2aee183a23bdfee) reports a triangulated, low-poly retro CRT with 3,742 triangles and non-overlapping UVs. The viewer displays approximately 1,900 vertices. Shell and screen glass have separate material maps. Texture maps are 4096 × 4096: color, roughness, metallic, OpenGL normal, and ambient occlusion. It was modeled in Blender and textured in Substance Painter and Photoshop.

The visible listing preview depicts a worn light-colored CRT housing with a deep rear enclosure, ventilation slots, and a swivel pedestal. A local preview image was not copied into the repository; the official listing is the visual reference.
