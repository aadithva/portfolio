## IKEA corner workstation

The photograph strongly matches the white **IKEA MICKE corner workstation**. The current India article number is **203.542.84**. The UK counterpart is **502.507.13**. Both current listings give a 100 × 100 cm footprint and 142 cm overall height. The corner plan, curved front opening, flanking shelves, white back panels, and rear cable management distinguish it from the straight MICKE desks. [IKEA India](https://www.ikea.com/in/en/p/micke-corner-workstation-white-20354284/), [IKEA UK](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/p/micke-corner-workstation-white-50250713/).

The height inference is supported by the [official 75 cm MICKE desk specification](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/p/micke-desk-white-30213076/) and the [IKEA India MICKE buying guide](https://www.ikea.com/in/en/files/pdf/c3/07/c307a741/micke.pdf). Page 2 of that guide lists the older corner height as 141 cm and the other desk surfaces as 75 cm.

For the model's proposed scale of 1.31 units per 75 cm, 100 cm is 1.7467 units and 142 cm is 2.4803 units. A 100 × 100 cm right-angle footprint has a 141.42 cm diagonal. That geometric calculation is not a manufacturer measurement of the curved front edge or clear leg opening.

Material references should remain restrained. IKEA specifies painted constructed boards with plastic edging for major panels, melamine-faced shelves, a powder-coated steel support, and painted steel notice boards. These support a satin white finish with thin edge seams, rather than marble, visible timber grain, or polished plastic. The photograph's large blank white areas behind the lower shelves should not automatically become cabinet doors; no handles are visible. [IKEA materials and product details](https://www.ikea.com/in/en/p/micke-corner-workstation-white-20354284/).

## Figma × Work Louder box

The official collaboration is the **Figma Creator Micro**, a customized first-generation Work Louder Creator Micro. Figma's announcement dated 7 December 2023 confirms **12 keys, two rotary encoders, and 48 shortcut assignments**. This is a compact shortcut pad. Do not substitute the controls or body of the later Creator Micro 2. [Figma's product announcement](https://www.figma.com/blog/figma-work-louder-custom-keyboard/).

The yellow box in the user's left shelf strongly matches this product's packaging. Figma's own article includes a closed yellow box with the Figma wordmark and a keyboard illustration, plus an opened-box photograph showing the device in its insert. The matching wordmark face, yellow exterior, shallow rectangular form, and fine dark top illustration are the useful identifiers. Actual retail-box dimensions and first-generation device dimensions were not established from a primary source, so retain editable proportions based on the photograph.

## Evidence limits

- No first-generation Creator Micro or retail-package dimensions were found in the primary sources checked. Unverified shipping-estimate pages were excluded.
- No manufacturer-provided downloadable Blender, CAD, or glTF model was established for either object.
- The gallery URLs are provenance references. Availability does not establish a redistribution license for the photographs.
